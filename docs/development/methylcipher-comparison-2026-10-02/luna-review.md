## What methylCIPHERv2 records

`list_clocks()` has five default columns: `clock_id`, `group_id`, `covariates`, `external`, and `tags` (`R/list_clocks.R:3-10`). Its expanded catalog adds group size, batch dependence, normalization scheme, scoring CpG count, and 12 training/publication descriptors: description, training cohort, tissues derived, array type trained, sample count, health status, minimum and maximum age plus age unit, training algorithm, sex distribution, and ancestry (`R/list_clocks.R:31-47`). The build's 13-column source codebook is the same list except the derived `n_cpgs` field is added by the package (`data-raw/sync.R:300-313,353-367`). `standout_summary` becomes `description`; blank, `NA`, and `NR` are collapsed to `NA` (`data-raw/sync.R:316-350`). That means unknown and not applicable are not distinguishable.

The metadata catalog is deeper than the expanded human-facing table. Its field registry supports `weights_format`, `computation_type`, `output_transform`, `normalization`, `imputation`, `intercept`, `covariates`, `recipe`, `components`, `shared`, `probe_sets`, code references/dependencies, definitions, licensing, and group routing (`data-raw/sync.R:70-118`). In the 147-entry runtime index, all entries have a normalization declaration, imputation policy, output-transform label, external flag, and citation count; 140 have computation type, weights format, and probe sets; 33 declare direct covariates, 14 direct clock inputs, and two a cross-sample calculation point. Normalization is `none` in 144, BMIQ in two, and quantile in one. Output transform is identity in 136, anti-transform in nine, and log-offset anti-transform in two. Policies are `omit` in 74, vendor reference mean in 70, and per-input in three. These machine fields are implementation recipe/provenance; the documentation-only training fields are not substitutes for them.

Citations are a separate relation with role and bibliographic fields; the catalog's PMID is the primary paper, whereas `mc_citations` can represent additional papers. The 147 catalog `license` values are mixed identifiers and descriptions (`journal-supp`, open/public categories, SPDX-like values, or unspecified), not evidence of the package license, copyright holder, redistribution rights for every asset, or author endorsement. The package lists authors and Yale copyright separately in `DESCRIPTION:5-23` and has a BSD-3 package license; neither that record nor a license label proves clock-author approval.

## Runtime contract supported by source

The user-facing contract is a row-per-sample beta matrix with CpGs in columns, plus optional sample phenotype with ID, age in years, and/or Female (0/1) for relevant models (`README.md:10-18`). `calc_clocks()` takes covariate aliases, normalization controls, and distinct clock-level and sample-level coverage thresholds (`R/calc_clocks.R:60-81`; `R/list_clocks.R:31-39`). It may return missing scores when coverage falls below thresholds (`R/calc_clocks.R:10-38`). It retains only sample IDs and covariates actually used in the result (`R/calc_clocks.R:40-45`).

Missingness semantics matter: fully absent CpGs use a shipped reference or are dropped according to each clock's policy; partially missing values in a present column are CpG-wise column-mean imputed (`README.md:121-124`). There may also be a second background panel used for quantile/BMIQ normalization, distinct from the scoring panel; coverage checks and defaults determine whether normalization runs (`R/calc_clocks.R:24-38`; `R/list_clocks.R:31-39`). Some scores are routed through sex-specific branches; composite scores can depend on other clock outputs. Principal-component models can consume a wide raw CpG panel even when the regression runs on a smaller number of components. Thus “number of CpGs,” “number of nonzero terms,” and “number of downstream components” are three different counts.

## Crosswalk review and cautions

The corrected crosswalk accounts for 131 public requestable upstream entries (104 mutual public targets, 25 unmatched public entries, and two related public entries) plus 16 hidden branches (eight linked sex-routed components, six unmatched routed components, and two internal cAge branches). The public mutual comparison denominator remains 104. The identity cautions below are important when using the metadata crosswalk as anything more than candidate discovery:

- Do not map methylCIPHER `DNAmADM`, `DNAmB2M`, `DNAmCystatinC_GrimAgeV1`, `DNAmGDF15`, `DNAmLeptin`, `DNAmPACKYRS`, `DNAmPAI1`, and `DNAmTIMP1` as exact versions of pyaging `grimage2*` records. The former cite GrimAge v1 (DOI `10.18632/aging.101684`, 2019) and the latter GrimAge2 (DOI `10.18632/aging.204434`, 2022); six codebook panel counts are one CpG higher than pyaging (for example ADM 186 vs 187 and B2M 91 vs 92), while Leptin and PAI-1 counts match. Root’s subsequent tensor-level audit found all eight feature sets, covariates, coefficients, and intercepts agree within float32 precision, supporting the same fitted surrogate family. Preserve this qualified identity, but do not erase citation provenance: the 2019 paper is the component origin and the 2022 GrimAge2 paper documents reuse. Feature-count difference is a representational/panel-definition issue to annotate, not evidence by itself of a different fitted clock. Full scoring-pipeline parity is still not established.
- `cAge` is a Bernabeu et al. 2023 model and has no canonical pyaging target in this crosswalk. Do not equate it with Han et al. 2020 or the unrelated pyaging clocks. Its `cAge_gt_20`/`cAge_le_20` records are internal routed branches, not standalone clocks.
- `Age_prediction` is the chronology component used internally by SystemsAge, not a returned SystemsAge score. `DNAmFitAge_*` and related sex branches similarly need the umbrella target plus branch/covariate semantics; do not count each sex-specific branch as an independent pyaging model.
- `Retroelement_Age450K` is a platform-specific related variant, not automatically the same variant as `retroelementagev1`.
- Treat every publication/year mismatch as a citation-role/version check before copying. Same family can cite a later paper while a component was inherited; conversely, same DOI can describe distinct transforms. A candidate construct mapping does not authorize copying paper-specific training variables.

Root's panel audit confirms 68 exact assay-panel matches; 32 panels cannot be compared from local upstream files (external packs/routed aliases); and four pyaging models have larger raw input sets because of normalization/background inputs (CellPopAge 2,543 vs 42 scoring terms; X/Y 45,315/2 vs 4,047/2; Dunedin 20,000 vs 173). All 19 numeric `n_features` differences are accounted for by covariates or normalization features, not missing clock features. Therefore do not interpret those raw count differences as score-model mismatches. Root's 90 `definition_or_value_difference` rows and 24 publication differences are not 114 confirmed errors: multiple are predictably different semantics, such as fitted algorithm versus serialized scoring, pyaging's broad population label versus a cohort list, and vocabulary normalization. The paired DOI/year differences on GrimAge V1 components remain a citation-role issue: tensor evidence supports the shared fitted proxy family, with a 2019 component origin and a 2022 reuse paper. Check cohort ages independently: participant ages at a repeated measure or phenotype outcome are not necessarily ages of the methylation samples used to fit the clock. Likewise, EPIC compatibility/validation cannot be relabeled EPIC training without fit evidence establishing it.

## Concrete audit findings to carry into the plan

- Nine GrimAge2 proxy records in `clock_metadata.json` declare `postprocess: "cox_to_years"` even though their corresponding classes return the input score unchanged. `src/pyaging/models/_models.py:2553-2721` confirms identity postprocessing across the affected proxy classes; PAI-1 is the exception and has no such metadata declaration. This is stale runtime metadata, not a discovered numerical scoring bug. Any consumer using metadata rather than the code may misstate score semantics; add cross-validation between declared transforms and model behavior to the metadata checks.
- Upstream HypoClock recipe declares `output_transform: "identity"` and computes a raw mean (`mc_catalog.json`, `HypoClock.recipe.score`; upstream source snapshot at the pin), while pyaging's `HypoClock.postprocess()` returns `1 - x` (`src/pyaging/models/_models.py:431-451`). Classify this as an unresolved score-orientation variant until original paper/author code is reviewed. It does not by itself establish which package is wrong. Do not copy the upstream direction or label both scores interchangeable.
- `tutorials/tutorial_rnaseq.ipynb` already runs tAge and tAgeMortality examples. The docs action is a discoverability/content refresh around existing examples, not adding duplicate examples.

## Recommended additions (schema and adoption order)

The schema proposal in `recommendations.json` ranks changes. Highest value is a structured, evidence-backed `feature_contract` that clarifies what is actually supplied to pyaging: raw probes, derived inputs, PCs, nonzero model terms, and any separate normalization/background panel. Preserve legacy `n_features` as the effective packaged predictor dimension during migration; never overwrite it with the raw probe count. Next add structured training descriptors rather than stuffing cohort detail into `population` or `notes`, and separate original-paper specification from pyaging's implemented runtime behavior. Explicitly keep “fit platform” and “supported/validated platform” as distinct concepts; the current controlled vocabulary defines `platform` as fitting or feature selection and excludes validation/compatibility-only assays (`clocks/metadata/controlled_vocabulary.json:11-12,65-66`).

For all new fields, use a nullable value plus an explicit status: `reported`, `inferred`, `unknown`, or `not_applicable`; do not use an empty list or zero to mean unknown. Attach evidence at field level (source URL/DOI or repository commit, locator, access/snapshot date, and status), not only a record-level citation. Reuse pyaging's evidence-ledger approach, which currently has 240 clock records and 16 reviewed evidence groups per record; don't import the upstream descriptor CSV as verified evidence. Validate enum values, units, ranges, required nested keys, and references; validate panel counts against actual model assets. Keep existing per-clock metadata serialization backwards-readable by making new objects optional and derive existing display fields from them at read time. Schema rollout is moderate effort: update controlled vocabulary/JSON validation, construction and HF serialization, docs/catalog UI, and metadata audit generation; no scoring behavior needs to change.

Documentation follow-up from the user's scope belongs in the same metadata-plan queue: replace the standalone proteomic-clocks page with links into the tutorial and per-clock notes, and improve discovery of the existing tAge/tAgeMortality examples in the bulk-RNA tutorial. Preserve clear input/output scales and cohort dependence. This is a content/navigation refresh, not a request to duplicate examples; it should not imply that any methylCIPHER comparison establishes license, endorsement, or validation.
